## What Google needs from Crewzy

Google discovers URLs, crawls their content, decides what to index and selects
results for individual searches. A technically accessible page can still remain
unindexed; an indexed page can still attract little traffic. Paid ads do not buy
better organic rankings. The work therefore combines technical eligibility,
clear buyer-focused pages, credible evidence and measurement—not keyword
registration. [How Google Search works](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/how-search-works)

Google ignores the meta-keywords tag. Useful wording belongs in the visible page,
its title, description, headings and descriptive internal links. Existing keyword
metadata is retained for compatibility, not presented as an SEO improvement.
[Supported metadata](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/special-tags)

Do not target payroll processing, rota scheduling, identity verification or
guaranteed legal compliance as supported Crewzy capabilities. Avoid dozens of
near-duplicate city or industry pages. Add an industry page only when it contains
distinct workflow evidence and useful answers. No customer names or testimonials
have been invented; the owner previously confirmed there are none to publish.

## Implemented in this candidate

- Four statically generated workflow pages with unique titles/descriptions,
  self-canonicals, original visible content, implementation steps, questions,
  clear limitations, pricing/demo links and related workflows.
- Four original practical guides plus `/resources/guides`: document expiry
  tracking, approved timesheets to client invoices, spreadsheet migration and
  leave requests/approvals. Each has a worked example, checklist/table, sources,
  visible Crewzy attribution/revision date and relevant product links.
- Unknown feature and guide slugs return 404 instead of unlimited thin pages.
- Shared visible breadcrumbs and matching BreadcrumbList JSON-LD. Guides also
  include truthful Article markup. The shared serializer escapes script delimiters.
  No publication date, customer quotation, rating or review is fabricated.
- A shared title template preserves Crewzy branding through nested layouts; the
  production audit caught and verified the correction.
- A clearer homepage search title, description, primary heading and introduction.
  The agreed social-share caption and image remain intact.
- Crawlable links from the footer and platform overview. New content uses the
  established typography, colours, navigation and responsive layout.
- A shared route inventory for the sitemap and rendered SEO/link audits: seventeen
  marketing URLs, excluding intentionally noindex legal pages and app URLs.
- Build-context noindex HTTP headers on marketing previews, in addition to the
  existing HTML directives. Production remains indexable. Robots.txt still allows
  crawling so exclusion directives can be read.
- Optional, validated public Google/Bing verification metadata. Empty defaults
  make no ownership claim; no account is automatically created or connected.
- The observed footer link accessibility correction, scoped to survive inherited
  page styles. Guide typography is scoped similarly, with semantic headings,
  labelled tables, keyboard-focus styles and mobile contents navigation.
- Supported Next flat ESLint configuration with ESLint 9.39.5, Node 24 in Netlify,
  and a repeatable `npm run verify:ci` covering both indexing contexts. No
  application dependency was upgraded; the existing lockfile resolves Next 16.3.4.
- Regression sources and broader rendered checks covering preview headers,
  verification tokens, initial HTML, article/breadcrumb identity, unknown-route 404s
  and route coverage. Automated checks passed; see the compliance report.

The sitemap intentionally does not manufacture `lastmod` timestamps on every
build. Legal indexing restrictions remain an explicit launch decision. Software
review stars and FAQ rich results have not been fabricated; the new questions are
visible buyer content. Breadcrumb markup describes the actual navigation.
[Breadcrumb guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/breadcrumb)

### Measurement specification

Measure acquisition on the marketing site and confirm attribution through the
handoff to `app.crewzy.io`. Verify session continuity between these subdomains;
configure cross-domain linking only where the actual domain/cookie flow requires
it. Keep development traffic out of production reporting.

- `page_view`: approved public marketing pages.
- `signup_click`: outbound Start free action; a micro-conversion, not a signup.
- `demo_contact_click`: opening the contact/email flow; not a received lead.
- `sign_up`: only after successful account creation in the app.
- A qualified demo/lead event: only after a real submitted request or confirmed
  sales record; never infer completion from a mailto click.

Do not send names, email addresses, document information or employee/HR data in
events or URLs. The authenticated app needs a separate instrumentation review;
do not enable broad automatic page collection across HR screens. No tracking code
or extra data sharing is enabled by this SEO change.

## First 90 days

1. **Release and establish measurement:** validate the candidate in CI/preview,
   review content/layout, publish with approval, verify the live HTML and headers,
   then check sitemap discovery and indexing. Complete GA4/Bing setup with the
   owner. Record the release date so future comparisons use the right baseline.
2. **Release the four completed guides:** document renewals, timesheet-to-invoice
   reconciliation, spreadsheet migration and leave approvals are in this candidate.
   Add genuine product screenshots and customer evidence as they become available.
   Maintain the content when the product or official guidance changes; revision
   dates should reflect real editorial changes.
3. **Earn relevant attention:** share useful demonstrations with existing founder
   communities and agency networks, maintain the genuine listings, and request
   honest customer feedback when actual customers exist. Avoid purchased links,
   fabricated reviews and mass promotional posting.
4. **Review every 28 days:** compare UK non-brand impressions/clicks, useful
   landing-page traffic, completed signups, qualified demos and eventual paid
   conversions. Compare like-for-like windows and separate brand searches from
   acquisition searches. Low samples are directional, not statistical proof.
5. **Expand from evidence:** improve pages that earn relevant impressions but
   weak engagement; investigate conversion friction before buying more traffic.
   Add further pages only when search/customer questions establish a useful need.
